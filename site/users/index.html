You play with mods?

Your Deadlock mods clash and break after game updates. A small note from the mod maker can fix that.

The note follows DiffMod, a set of rules for mod makers and mod managers. The mod stays as it is, and the note comes next to it.

You cannot use it yet. Mod makers must add the notes first, and mod managers (apps that add mods for you) must learn to read them. You can push for it today. To hear when it works, join the DiffMod Discord.

What to do

  1. Copy this message:

    Hi! Could you add a DiffMod companion to your mod? It is one small .patch file next to the .vpk. Mod managers can use it to merge your mod with other mods and to repair it after game updates. Your VPK stays as it is. Guide: https://diffmod.github.io/diffmod-spec/mod-makers/

    The message uses the words that mod makers know. You do not need to know them.

  2. Post it under the mod on GameBanana, or send it to the mod maker on Discord. Do it for each HUD or menu mod that you use.

  3. Star DiffMod on GitHub. Each star shows mod makers that players want notes.

Questions

Why do my mods break?

Think of the game as a book. Each page holds the lines that draw one part of your screen. A mod wants to change one line on a page. But today it must swap the whole page for its own copy.

  • Two mods. Both mods want to swap the same page. The game can take only one copy. The other mod breaks.
  • Game updates. The game gets a new page. The mod still swaps in its old copy. The new things from the update are gone, and your screen can break.
How does the note fix it?

The note says: "On this page, change only this." Mod makers call the note a companion.

  • Your mod manager reads the notes of two mods and makes both changes on one page. Both mods work.
  • After a game update, your mod manager takes the new page and makes the changes again.
What do I get?

Once your mods come with notes and your mod manager reads them:

  • You can use mods together that broke each other before.
  • After a game update, your mod manager shows which mods are OK and which are not.
  • Your mod manager offers to repair many broken mods. You do not wait for the mod maker.
  • You keep what the update adds, and you keep the mod.
  • Your mod manager can show you what a mod changes before you add it.
When can I use this?

When your mod manager reads the notes and your mods come with them. Neither is true yet. Each message to a mod maker and each star on GitHub makes it come sooner. We list mod managers that read the notes on this page when they come out. To hear when that happens, join the DiffMod Discord.

Do I need a mod manager?

No. Your mods work with no mod manager, as they do today. But only a mod manager can fix mods for you.

I add mods by hand. Do I need to do anything?

No. If a mod comes with a note, it's only useful for a manager, which usually downloads the mods for you for example from GameBanana. If you add mods by hand, you can ignore the note, it won't benefit you.

Can Valve ban me for mods with notes?

Very unlikely. The game sees the same kind of mod as today. Your mod manager uses the notes on your PC, before the game starts, and then gives the game a normal mod. In Dota 2, a mod tool called Minify already builds its mods the same way, and its maker says that no one has been banned for them. Still, only Valve decides, and nobody can promise what Valve does.

Is the note safe?

Yes. It's a small plain text file. It cannot run on your PC. You can open it in Notepad and see each change that the mod makes.

What if two mods still do not work together?

That happens if both mods change the very same thing. Then your mod manager leaves out the change of one mod and tells you which mod. To keep that mod instead, change the order of your mods.

Does this work for all mods?

No, and most mods do not need it. Version 1 of the rules covers Deadlock mods that move, resize, restyle, add or hide things on your screen, such as the HUD, the shop and the menus. Those clash the most. The note does not cover pictures, skins, models or sounds. Those mostly swap things of their own, so they rarely clash.

A mod that changes your HUD and also swaps models or sounds still gets the help for its HUD part. The rest works as it does today.

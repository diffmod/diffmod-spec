You play with mods?

Mods break? Ask your mod makers for a DiffMod note.

What you get

  • You can use mods together that broke each other before.
  • After a game update, your mod manager shows which mods are OK and which are not.
  • Many broken mods get a fix on your PC in a moment. You do not wait for the mod maker.
  • New things from a game update do not hide behind an old mod.
  • You can see what a mod changes before you add it.

You get this only if the mod comes with a note and your mod manager reads it. A mod manager is an app that adds mods to your game for you.

Why mods break

Think of the game as a book. Each page holds the lines that draw one part of your screen. A mod wants to change one line on a page. But today it must swap the whole page for its own copy.

  • Two mods. Both mods want to swap the same page. The game can take only one copy. The other mod breaks.
  • Game updates. The game gets a new page. The mod still swaps in its old copy. The new things from the update are gone, and your screen can break.

The fix: a small note

With DiffMod, a mod comes with a small note. The note says: "On this page, change only this." Mod makers call the note a companion.

  • Your mod manager reads the notes of two mods and makes both changes on one page. Both mods work.
  • After a game update, your mod manager takes the new page and makes the changes again.
  • With no mod manager, your mods work as they do today.

What to do

  1. If a mod has no note, ask its maker for one. You can do this today. The message is below.
  2. If you add a mod by hand, keep all the parts of the download together. The note is the small part with .patch at the end of its name.
  3. Use a mod manager that reads DiffMod notes. No mod manager does that yet. We list them here when they do.

Copy this message and send it to the mod maker:

Hi! Could you add a DiffMod companion to your mod? It is one small .patch file next to the .vpk. Mod managers use it to merge your mod with other mods and to repair it after game updates. Your VPK stays as it is. Guide: https://source2-diffmod.org/mod-makers/

The message uses the words that mod makers know. You do not need to know them.

Questions

Do I need a mod manager?

No. Your mods work with no mod manager, as they do today. But only a mod manager can fix mods for you.

Is the note safe?

Yes. It is plain text. It cannot run on your PC. You can open it in Notepad and see each change that the mod makes.

What if two mods still do not work together?

That happens if both mods change the very same thing. Your mod manager tells you which two mods. It keeps the mod that is first in your mod list. Move the other mod up to keep that one.

Does this work for all mods?

No, and most mods do not need it. Version 1 of the rules covers Deadlock mods that change your screen, such as the HUD and menus, because those clash the most. Skin, model and sound mods mostly swap pages of their own, so they rarely clash.
